Add `@taucad/bambu/plate`: pre-rendered binary glTF models of the four X1-Carbon build plates (Cool, Engineering, High Temp/Smooth PEI and gold Textured PEI) and the X1C hotend tip, with `bambuX1cPlates`, `bambuX1cHotend` and `bambuPlateForBedType`. Each descriptor carries the model URL, its glTF Y-up metre convention, the plate-frame bounds, the surface colour and finish, and every bed-type name a sliced file may record.
