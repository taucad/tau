Ship the copyright notices and licence texts of the third-party libraries the `cadquery-ocp-novtk` wheels bundle beside OCCT (Little CMS, Fontconfig, FreeType, libpng, libjpeg-turbo, LibTIFF, libwebp, OpenJPEG, OpenEXR and Imath, jxrlib, LibRaw, liblzma, Zstandard, libuuid, zlib, libdeflate, LERC, OpenJPH, libc++, FreeImage under FIPL-1.0 and libgomp under GPL-3.0-or-later with the GCC Runtime Library Exception), with a per-target inventory in `notices/cadquery-ocp-novtk/NOTICE`.
