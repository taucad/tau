Carry the remote's head for each branch in the projection (D4). `RevisionBranchFacet.remoteHead` is the revision the remote holds for that branch, from the last fetch or push, or from the queued entry's lease on a device that opened offline. It is absent while there is no remote or the remote has never had the branch. History draws "Backed up to here" above it.
