`ExportPane` exports one format at a time, chosen from a single format picker instead of a grid of toggle chips. The picker lists the common formats (STEP, 3MF, GLB, OBJ, STL, USDZ) under the BREP and Mesh headings, with a "More formats" row that opens the rest in a searchable nested picker: a popover beside the list on wide screens, a stacked drawer on mobile. The selected format's settings appear under a collapsed "Export options" section. The pane no longer offers zipping several formats.
