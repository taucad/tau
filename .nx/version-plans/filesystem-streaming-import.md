filesystem: minor
---

A new `@taucad/filesystem/importer` entry imports a project without the page reading its bytes (charter D12). A source is a zip `Blob` read by range (its central directory, ZIP64 included), a list of `File`s with their paths, a directory handle, or a downloaded archive spooled into a `Blob` against its declared size; `planImport` lists it under the injected path policy, pruning what the project never versions while it walks, skipping symlinks, refusing zip-slip paths and paths one filesystem would merge, and enforcing one limits policy (`importLimits`: 1 GiB archive, 1 GiB expanded, 100 MiB per file, a ratio of 100, a 1 KiB path and 50,000 entries) as it lists and as it decodes, each refusal an `ImportRefusedError` with its reason. `decodeImportEntry` inflates one entry into a buffer of its declared size, checks the zip CRC, and computes its git blob id and SHA-256 in the same pass. `stageImport` writes the decoded entries once into `.tau-staging/import-<operation>/` in changesets of at most 256 files or 32 MiB, recording each batch in a ledger, and resumes a staging of the same source after a crash. `commitPendingProjectDirectory` takes a `staging` locator and commits the project by moving the staged files into place, with `tau.json` written last; `WorkspaceFileService.importStaging(scope)` gives the importer its storage root. OPFS no longer sweeps an import's staging as a dead realm's.
