Phone and tablet touch pass over the Workbench panes (charter W6). Under a coarse pointer, `@taucad/viewer`'s parameter fields take the 40px touch row with 16px input type, a boolean parameter's switch hit area spans its row (reset and hint buttons stay their own targets above it, and the name labels the switch), and portaled select options take the touch row too. `@taucad/ui`'s `ModifiedIndicator` shows its reset icon without hover and grows to a 24px target under a coarse pointer. `@taucad/workbench-panes`' parameter group actions are named ("Rename <group>") and shown on focus and on coarse pointers, the empty Parameters pane explains what to ask for, and export option fields follow the same touch row. In the model tree, a hidden part is labelled "Hidden" (its row button is named "<part>, hidden") and keeps its Show action in view, row actions show under a coarse pointer as well as `hover: none`, and the empty tree says when parts appear. Kinematics joint values and its filter take the touch row under a coarse pointer, entry header actions in Parameters and Kinematics show under a coarse pointer, and the Kinematics pane with no geometry units says when joints appear. The Share pane's checkbox labels are 24px targets under a coarse pointer. The model tree filter takes the touch row under a coarse pointer, and the Share frame heading carries `data-slot='share-pane-title'` so a host that titles the pane itself can hide it. Under a coarse pointer a compact model tree row is 32px so its part button reaches 24px, and the Export destination checkbox labels are at least 24px tall.
