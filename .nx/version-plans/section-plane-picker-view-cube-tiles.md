The section plane picker beside the view cube is drawn as pieces of the cube: flat, unlit tiles in the cube's face tone with their XY, XZ and YZ labels printed on the face in the cube's font, turning and foreshortening with the view and never mirrored. Each tile's band always carries the muted colour of its plane's normal axis. Hovering a tile washes its face with that colour, the selected plane's tile is filled with it, and hovering the selected tile deepens it. The tiles follow the viewer's theme like the cube does.
