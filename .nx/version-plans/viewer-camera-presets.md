Camera presets and the input router. `@taucad/viewer/graphics/three/controls/camera-presets` exports the six mouse presets (P1 to P6) and the trackpad mapping (P7) of the camera research as binding tables, with the pure resolvers `resolveDrag`, `resolveWheel` and `resolveCameraMapping`; a button and modifier combination a preset does not list does nothing. `ThreeViewerProperties`, `CadViewer` and `ModelViewer` take an optional `cameraInput` (`preset`, `inputDevice`, `zoomToCursor`, `orbitAboutCursor`, `invertZoom`) and `gizmoStyle` (ids in `@taucad/viewer/graphics/three/controls/viewport-gizmo-styles`). With a camera input the capture-phase router beside the controls applies the preset to every press and wheel step, waits until a right-button press travels past 4 px before it moves the camera so a click stays a click, treats a macOS Ctrl + click as the secondary click, and keeps a Ctrl + wheel inside the viewer from zooming the page. Without one, every binding is as before.
