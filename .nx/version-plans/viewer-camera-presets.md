Camera presets as data: `@taucad/viewer/graphics/three/controls/camera-presets` exports the six mouse presets (P1 to P6) and the trackpad mapping (P7) of the camera research as binding tables, with the pure resolvers `resolveDrag`, `resolveWheel` and `resolveCameraMapping`. A button and modifier combination a preset does not list does nothing.
