viewer: minor
---

The kinematics actor owns the viewer's part-drag gesture. `kinematicsMachine` now has states (`idle → pressed → dragging (steady ⇄ coalescing) → idle`, and `dragging → cancelled → idle` after Escape) and accepts `pressPart`, `movePointer`, `releasePointer`, `losePointer`, `interruptGesture` and `cancelGesture`. A press that it claims starts the drag adapter the press carries (`KinematicsDragAdapterLogic`, callback logic that keeps the camera, scene root, drag plane and DOM elements in its closure); pointer targets coalesce for `kinematicsDragMoveWindow` (16 ms) on the actor's clock before one solve, in place of one solve per animation frame. The machine emits `poseSettled { unitId, revision }` once when a drag ends, kept on release or restored by a cancel, from the gesture or from `dragEnd`, `dragCancel` and a disarming `setDragEnabled` alike. New exports: `kinematicsDragThresholdPx`, `kinematicsDragMoveWindow`, `kinematicsEventTypes`, `kinematicsIgnoredEvents`, `selectKinematicsDragging` and the types `KinematicsDragAdapterCommand`, `KinematicsDragAdapterLogic`, `KinematicsGesture` and `KinematicsEmitted`; the context gains `gesture` and `dragAdapter`.

Behaviour changes: clearing a unit's mechanism while it is pressed or dragged ends the press and gives the camera back (the viewer clears a unit as it unmounts it, which replaces the hook's unmount cleanup), and a press whose unit is disarmed before it travels the threshold holds the camera until its release without capturing the pointer. `dragStart`, `dragMove`, `dragEnd` and `dragCancel` are unchanged.

Breaking: `kinematics-drag-controls.ts` and its `beginKinematicsDragGesture` are removed; `useKinematicsDragControls` moved to `kinematics-drag-controls-adapter.ts` beside `createKinematicsDragAdapter`. `kinematics-drag-math.ts` no longer exports `kinematicsDragThresholdPx` or `hasExceededKinematicsDragThreshold`.
