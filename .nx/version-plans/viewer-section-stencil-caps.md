viewer: minor

`@taucad/viewer` draws section caps with the stencil buffer on WebGL and WebGPU, instead of slicing every visible mesh, unioning and differencing the cut polygons with Clipper and triangulating them on the main thread at each step of a drag. Each source's meshes count how often they cross the cut, a polygon on the cut plane fills each owner's cap where the count is odd, and an overlap bit draws the red and yellow overlap stripes on exactly the pixels where two owners' solids hold the cut. The cut edges are outlined on the GPU by a band on each source's own surface. A drag frame's main-thread cost depends on the cut pieces and the meshes, not on their triangle count, and certifying a frame whose cuts and sources are unchanged returns early. The caps draw as an overlay pass after post-processing restores depth, so they are not tone-mapped or ambient-occluded.

Sections are graded per source rather than refused; see `viewer-tolerant-section-caps`. A source's closure census larger than `maxSynchronousBvhTriangles` runs in the section worker; a worker error retries once on a new worker and then caps that source per loop with the code `topology-unavailable`, which replaces `bvh-unavailable`. No main-thread census runs in its place. The CPU contour extractor `extractSectionContours`, the cap overlap worker and the vertex-coloured stripe material are removed; the exact cap region, overlap and polygon boolean modules remain for consumers that ask for exact results. The new `graphics/three/utils/section-cap-stencil-debug` subpath exports where the latest cap counts are published while Tau debugging is on.
