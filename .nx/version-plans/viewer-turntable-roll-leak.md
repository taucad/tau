In Turntable, a rolled view restored on reload, adopted from a saved view record or reported by another sender is straightened: the view direction stays and the up is the scene up again, so the next drag turns about the world up instead of a sideways axis. The straightening of an automatic restore is itself automatic, so the view record does not save it. A view straight along the scene up keeps its roll, and Free orbit keeps every roll as before.
