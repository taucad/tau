# Acceptable Use Policy

*Effective date: November 8, 2026*

This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It sets out what you may not do with Tau. Breaking it can lead to content being removed or your account being suspended or closed.

## Don't

- Break the law, or help anyone else to.
- Infringe other people's rights: copyright, trademarks, patents, trade secrets or privacy. Share only what you have the right to share.
- Harass, threaten, defame or impersonate people, or create fake accounts. Pseudonymous accounts are fine as long as the contact and billing details you give us are accurate.
- Attack Tau or anyone else: no malware, no probing or breaking security, no scraping or automated access beyond what the API allows, no circumventing limits, and no reselling access without our agreement.
- Share sexual content, graphic violence or content that promotes hatred against people because of who they are.
- Spam: unsolicited advertising, phishing or bulk unwanted messages.

## Designs and AI

- Tau's AI can be wrong. Check its output before you make, sell or rely on anything, and don't try to manipulate it into producing prohibited content. Our AI providers' usage policies also apply to AI features and may block some requests.
- Don't design or make weapons, or parts of weapons, that are illegal to make or own where you are — for example untraceable firearms or their components — or help anyone else do so.
- Some designs, such as aerospace, defence and dual-use items, are controlled by export laws. You are responsible for knowing whether yours are, and for not sharing or exporting them in breach of those laws or sanctions.

## Child safety

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material or any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers children. We remove it, close the account, and report it to the authorities.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a violation to support@tau.new with a link and what is wrong. Depending on what we find, we may warn you, remove or restrict content, suspend or close the account, or report the matter to law enforcement. We are not obliged to monitor Tau, but we may. If you think we acted in error, reply to the notice or write to support@tau.new with your reasons and we will look again.
