# Pebble Soap Dish

A sink dish: soft plan, open bowl, five drain holes.

JSCAD is the showcase because `hull` of eight spheres is the whole outside. OpenSCAD can approximate it with `minkowski`, slowly. A BRep kernel would loft or fillet a plan and still read as a rounded box. The curves here are mesh from the start, which is the JSCAD trade.

Parameters: `length`, `width`, `bead`, `wall`, `drainDiameter`, `segments`.
