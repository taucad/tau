# Parts Bin

A desktop organizer: rounded rectangular shell, constant wall, scoop in the front lip.

OpenSCAD is the showcase because the whole part is a difference of two extruded offsets plus one cylinder, and every dimension is a top-level customizer assignment. That is the Thingiverse model shape. A BRep fillet or a voxel lattice is the long way around a box a printer can make in an hour.

Parameters: `length`, `width`, `height`, `wall`, `floor_thickness`, `corner_radius`, `scoop_radius`.
