# KiCad libraries

The footprints and 3D models in `src/assets/kicad/` are derived from the official KiCad libraries
(<https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries>), as mirrored by `kicad-mod-cache.tscircuit.com`. The footprints were
converted to circuit JSON and the STEP models tessellated to GLB by `build-kicad-pack.ts`.
