# Open-end compression spring

A true helical round-wire spring with individually adjustable outer diameter, wire diameter, pitch and integral coil count.

Round-wire helix with open, unground ends. Free geometry only; spring rate, stress, fatigue, preload and solid-height service limits are not certified. Integer turns preserve the declared datum and end orientation.

All dimensions are millimetres. Edit main.ts and its local model.ts or use Tau parameters. Domains and qualified combinations are in parameters.ts. Defaults and every declared domain boundary/discrete value are tested by main.geospec.ts through STEP and GLB. These finite checks do not prove every intermediate real-valued combination.
